Build ReliefRoute

A programmable USDC voucher rail for disaster relief, NGO grants, and employee-benefit disbursements.

Recommended winner

ReliefRoute — money that can only be spent where it helps

An organization issues a purpose-bound voucher to a beneficiary. It can be redeemed at approved merchants by QR code, while an on-chain policy rejects cash-out or unauthorized transfers. The organizer gets a live audit trail without collecting a central payments ledger.

Why it wins: it makes Solana’s technical primitives visible in one 90-second demo, addresses a real coordination problem, and avoids the already-crowded agent-wallet/escrow market.

48-hour MVP

  1. Issuer dashboard: create a food, medicine, or education campaign; allocate demo USDC vouchers.
  2. Anchor transfer-hook program: enforce merchant allowlist, campaign expiry, and per-beneficiary balance cap.
  3. Beneficiary wallet page with QR redemption; merchant PWA scans and settles.
  4. Public transparency dashboard: issued, redeemed, rejected policy attempts, and remaining balance.

Demo moment: send a “food” voucher to a wallet, successfully spend it at an approved grocery merchant, then show the same voucher transfer to an unapproved wallet failing on-chain. That makes the policy real—not a frontend promise.

Why Solana, specifically

Token-2022 transfer hooks invoke custom program logic on transfers, which supports allowlists and other transfer policy. Fast, low-cost stablecoin settlement makes many small redemptions practical. A normal database can record vouchers, but cannot give funders, merchants, and beneficiaries one independently verifiable settlement state.

Blunt bear case

  • ReliefRoute: aid organizations move slowly and may require compliance, custody, and local cash-out partners. Keep the first version as a transparent, closed-loop voucher layer—not a regulated remittance service.
  • MilestonePay: without an acquisition channel, it is a prettier escrow UI in a crowded space.
  • Agent Spend Policy SDK: it will look derivative beside existing Solana agent-wallet, marketplace, and x402 work unless your policy model is radically narrower.

Build choices that keep it shippable

Use Token-2022 + a minimal TransferHook rather than inventing a stablecoin or writing a generic payment protocol. Start with a campaign-specific demo token on devnet; describe USDC integration as the production path, because USDC itself is not your programmable mint. Make merchant enrollment admin-controlled for the demo. Do not claim privacy or regulatory approval.

Research evidence

  • Solana’s Token-2022 transfer-hook documentation describes custom transfer-time enforcement and client account resolution: Solana docs.
  • Transfer hooks are explicitly used for allowlists, royalty enforcement, transfer logging, and rate limits: Solana Bytes guide.
  • Solana positions stablecoins for fast, 24/7 cross-border and B2B settlement: Solana institutional payments.
  • Agent payments are now a documented Solana pattern, and active repos already provide wallets, escrow, marketplaces, and x402: Solana agentic payments, AgentWallet, AgenC.
